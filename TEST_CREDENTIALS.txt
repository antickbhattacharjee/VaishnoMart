6. DUAL-OTP VERIFICATION & TESTING CODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 1 - Store Pickup OTP (Vendor -> Delivery Rider):
- When an order is accepted and assigned, the Store Pickup Code is displayed 
  directly in the green banner on the Vendor's Order Details page (/vendor/orders/{id}).
- The Delivery Rider must enter this code at /agent/deliveries/current to confirm pickup.
- Universal Demo Code: In local development, you can also enter "123456".

Stage 2 - Customer Delivery Confirmation OTP (Customer -> Delivery Rider):
- When an order is Out for Delivery, the 6-digit confirmation OTP is displayed 
  in the yellow alert banner on the Customer's Order Tracking page (/customer/orders/{id}).
- The Delivery Rider must enter this OTP upon handing over the parcel to complete delivery.
- Universal Demo Code: In local development, you can also enter "123456".
